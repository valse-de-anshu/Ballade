# 🎥 Your Minimal MPV Rice Setup

Welcome to your sleek, unixporn-approved `mpv` configuration! This document contains all the shortcuts, tricks, and explanations you need for everyday media consumption.

## ⌨️ Essential Keyboard Shortcuts

Your setup is fully keyboard-friendly. Because you have `uosc` and `autoload` installed, navigation is powerful:

### ⏪ Playback & Navigation
*   **`Space`**: Play / Pause
*   **`Enter`**: Toggle Fullscreen
*   **`Esc`**: Exit Fullscreen
*   **`>`** or **`N`**: Skip to the **Next** video in the folder (Thanks to the autoload script)
*   **`<`** or **`P`**: Go to the **Previous** video in the folder

### ⏩ Seeking
*   **`Right Arrow`**: Skip forward 5 seconds
*   **`Left Arrow`**: Skip backward 5 seconds
*   **`Shift + Right Arrow`**: Skip forward 30 seconds
*   **`Shift + Left Arrow`**: Skip backward 30 seconds

### 🔊 Audio & Subtitles
*   **`Up Arrow` / `Down Arrow`**: Adjust volume up/down (You can also use your **mouse wheel**)
*   **`A`**: Cycle through available Audio tracks (e.g., dual audio anime or movies)
*   **`S`**: Cycle through available Subtitle tracks
*   **`V`**: Toggle subtitle visibility (hide/show subtitles)

### 🖱️ Mouse Controls
*   **`Right Click`**: Opens the beautiful **uosc Context Menu** (Use this to manually select specific audio/sub track names, load external files, or change UI settings).
*   **`Hover Bottom`**: Reveals the symmetrical controls and the timeline with thumbnail previews.

## 🪄 Everyday Tricks & Features

1.  **Centered Pop-up Mode**: Your mpv is configured to act as a floating pop-up. It ignores system window managers and always opens perfectly in the dead center of your screen without bulky window borders.
2.  **Drag & Drop**: You don't have to open mpv from the terminal. You can just drag any video file, or even an entire folder of videos, straight into the mpv window.
3.  **YouTube / Web Video Streaming**: Because your config is primed for `yt-dlp`, you can drag and drop a YouTube URL from your browser straight into the mpv window, and it will play ad-free in 1080p high quality!

## 🎨 What are Shaders? (Are they Themes?)

You asked if **shaders** are like themes. **No, shaders are not themes!**

*   **Themes** (like your `uosc` setup) change the Buttons, Colors, and UI Layout.
*   **Shaders** act like camera lenses or real-time Photoshop filters for the *video itself*. They run directly on your GPU to enhance the video quality.

### ✨ Pre-Installed Shaders & Hotkeys

I have installed the ultimate shader pack for you, complete with easy hotkeys to toggle them on the fly:

*   **`CTRL + 1`**: **Anime4K** (For Anime & Cartoons)
    *   *What it does:* Uses real-time neural networks to make blurry or pixelated anime lines look incredibly sharp, like native 4K.
*   **`CTRL + 3`**: **FSRCNNX** (For Live-Action Movies/Shows/Videos)
    *   *What it does:* Restores textures and makes low-resolution real-life videos look crisp and modern.
*   **`CTRL + 4`**: **Dream Glow** (Custom Built!)
    *   *What it does:* Adds a soft, foggy, glowing bloom effect to the brightest parts of the video, creating a hazy, euphoric, dream-like atmosphere. Perfect for aesthetic edits or retro videos!
*   **`CTRL + 2`**: **Clear All Shaders**
    *   *What it does:* Instantly turns off all shaders and resets the video to its normal, unedited state.
